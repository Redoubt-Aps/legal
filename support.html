What to Include

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • The name of the app you're using
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • The app version (shown in the app's settings screen)
  • A description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot

Subscriptions & Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through your App Store account.

  • Manage or cancel a subscription: open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Restore purchases: use the "Restore purchases" option inside the app.
  • Refunds: refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
